## Detailed Game Breakdown

---

### 1. WordWeave (The Cozy Waffle)
*Inspired by: Waffle, NYT Strands, Scrabble*

- **The Hook**: A daily 5×5 grid with 6 intersecting five-letter words (3 horizontal, 3 vertical) with 4 empty corner gaps.
- **Rules**:
  1. All letters are already on the grid, but scrambled.
  2. Players drag and swap any two letters.
  3. Every board can be solved in 10 swaps. You have a budget of 15 swaps.
  4. Instant color cues:
     - **Pastel Sage Green**: Correct letter in the correct position.
     - **Buttercream Yellow**: Letter belongs in that row or column, but in a different slot.
     - **Muted Cream**: Letter does not belong in this row or column.
  5. Earn 1 star for every remaining swap (up to 5 stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐).
- **Why It Works**:
  - Waffle's swap mechanic has proven viral stickiness because players never get "stuck" with empty text boxes—all the pieces are already on the board.
  - The tactile satisfaction of dragging and snapping letter tiles into place feels like physical wooden board game pieces.
  - Re-theming with cozy word banks (botany, kitchen, cozy hobbies, warm drinks) creates a comforting, relaxing morning ritual.

### 2. HueSnap (Pastel Color Memory)
*Inspired by: Dialed.gg, I Love Hue*

- **The Hook**: Humans have notoriously imperfect color memory. Test how accurately your brain can remember a soft pastel hue.
- **Rules**:
  1. Round begins: A cozy pastel circle (e.g., matcha green, dusty lavender, apricot milk) appears for 2.5 seconds.
  2. The circle vanishes, replaced by a blank canvas and two tactile sliders:
     - **Hue** (Color wheel slider: 0° to 360°)
     - **Softness / Lightness** (Pastel blend slider)
  3. Dial in your guess and press **"Lock It In"**.
  4. The original swatch slides beside your guess to reveal the comparison.
  5. Scored from `0.0` to `10.0` points per round (5 rounds total, max 50 points).
- **Personality & Microcopy**:
  - `9.5+`: *"Are you a hummingbird? That's suspiciously accurate."*
  - `7.0 - 9.4`: *"Your brain took a proper snapshot."*
  - `<5.0`: *"Did you have your sunglasses on?"*
- **Why It Works**:
  - Universal appeal: No language, age, or cultural barrier.
  - Takes under 45 seconds to finish all 5 rounds.
  - Perfect for 1-click friend challenges: *"I scored 44.2/50 on today's HueSnap. Beat that!"*

### 3. Ballpark (The Cozy Napkin Math Game)
*Inspired by: Fermi.gg, Chronophoto*

- **The Hook**: Quick gut estimation of intriguing, heartwarming, or surprising real-world numbers without Googling.
- **Daily Prompts (Examples)**:
  - *"How many coffee beans are ground to brew a single double-shot espresso?"* (Answer: ~120 to 140 beans)
  - *"How many books are housed in the Library of Congress?"* (Answer: ~39,000,000)
  - *"How many hours does an average domestic cat spend sleeping across a 15-year life?"* (Answer: ~78,800 hours)
- **Rules**:
  1. 3 questions per day.
  2. Use a smooth exponential slider or direct numeric keypad.
  3. Built-in "Napkin Scratchpad" for quick arithmetic.
  4. Scored by error ratio:
     - Exact = `1.00×`
     - Within 20% = `1.20×`
     - Off by 3x = `3.00×`
  5. Post-guess reveal displays the math breakdown and where you rank against other players.
- **Why It Works**:
  - Sparks genuine intellectual curiosity and water-cooler discussions.
  - Lower stakes and much warmer than dry math quizzes.

### 4. BentoSort (Satisfying Cozy Organization)
*Inspired by: A Little to the Left, Townscaper, Unpacking*

- **The Hook**: A mini spatial puzzle satisfying the human urge to organize and declutter.
- **Rules**:
  1. You are presented with a 3×3 or 4×4 wooden bento box containing cute pastel items (tea cup, croissant, succulent, fountain pen, candle, yarn ball).
  2. A gentle cozy card gives 2 aesthetic harmony hints:
     - *"Warm pastries must sit along the top row."*
     - *"Plants prefer sitting next to porcelain."*
     - *"No two hot drinks can share a column."*
  3. Drag and swap tiles until all harmony criteria glow soft sage green.
  4. No countdown timer—pure zen, relaxing chimes on each tile snap.
- **Why It Works**:
  - Extremely soothing, visual ASMR.
  - Appeals directly to the massive cozy gaming audience on TikTok and Instagram.

### 5. StepStone (Cozy Semantic Association)
*Inspired by: Semantle, Redactle, NYT Connections*

- **The Hook**: Build a 4-step word bridge between two seemingly unrelated cozy concepts.
- **Rules**:
  1. Daily goal: Connect **HONEY** ➡️ **SWEATER**.
  2. Players type intermediate words:
     - Step 1: `HONEY` ➡️ `TEA` (Strong connection!)
     - Step 2: `TEA` ➡️ `WARMTH`
     - Step 3: `WARMTH` ➡️ `BLANKET`
     - Step 4: `BLANKET` ➡️ `SWEATER` (Target reached!)
  3. Scored on shortest path and associative tightness.
- **Status**: Chosen as the flagship. The full design (word-similarity judging, golf-style par, river scene) lives in [05-stepstone-game-design-and-architecture.md](05-stepstone-game-design-and-architecture.md) and supersedes the rough rules above. Closest competitor: Chainle (see the competitor deep-dive).
- **Why It Works**:
  - Lateral thinking exercise that feels creative rather than stressful.
  - Encourages playful vocabulary experimentation.

---

### 6. Kalimba (Gentle Ear & Melodic Memory)
*Inspired by: Simon Says, Bandle, Animal Crossing Gyroids*

- **The Hook**: A pocket music-box game.
- **Rules**:
  1. A virtual 5-tine pastel kalimba plays a gentle 3-to-5 note chime melody using the pentatonic scale (which always sounds harmonious).
  2. The player taps the tines to recreate the tune.
  3. Progressively adds 1 note each round across 4 rounds.
  4. Synthesized purely via browser Web Audio API—zero MP3 downloads.
- **Why It Works**:
  - Pentatonic chimes are inherently relaxing and impossible to sound discordant.
  - Very quick, meditative loop.

## Recommended Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Flagship Minigame (Pick 1 to Master First)
- **Top Recommendation: `HueSnap` or `WordWeave`**.
  - `HueSnap` can be built and polished in 1–2 days, has zero content generation bottlenecks, and is instantly viral due to its visual beauty.
  - `WordWeave` is the ultimate daily retention powerhouse with immense replayability.

### Phase 2: Multi-Game Cozy Hub ("Game.io" / Cozy Arcade)
- Once the first game is live with stats, streak tracking, and share cards, add a cozy game picker menu so users can play their daily 3-minute cozy routine.
