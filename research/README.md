# Game WebApp Research & Design Playbook

Research on modern viral web games (**Krillion**, **Fermi.gg**, **Dialed.gg**, **Waffle**, **Chainle**, and **NYT Games**), why they succeed, and how to build a lightweight, comforting web game. The chosen flagship is **StepStone**.

---

## Research Index

1. **[Competitor Deep-Dive](01-competitor-deep-dive.md)**
   - Breakdown of Krillion, Fermi.gg, Dialed.gg, Waffle, Chainle, and NYT Games.
   - Input types, round structure, gradient scoring, and thematic hooks.
   - Feature comparison table.

2. **[Consumer Psychology & Retention](02-consumer-psychology-and-retention.md)**
   - Zero-friction first touch.
   - Gradient scoring (meters of depth, ratio multiples, points out of 50) vs. binary win/loss.
   - Daily ritual and habit stacking.
   - Personality-driven microcopy.
   - Spoiler-free share cards and challenge links.

3. **[UI/UX & Aesthetic Design Guide](03-ui-ux-aesthetic-guide.md)**
   - Cozy pastel color tokens, tactile buttons, typography, touch targets.
   - Web Audio sound ideas.

4. **[Game Concepts & Creative Ideas](04-game-concepts-and-ideas.md)**
   - Six lightweight minigame concepts: WordWeave, HueSnap, Ballpark, BentoSort, StepStone, Kalimba.

5. **[StepStone Game Design & Architecture](05-stepstone-game-design-and-architecture.md)**
   - The flagship: concept, rules, scoring, word-similarity judging, data pipeline, tech stack, and roadmap.

---

## Key Takeaway

The games that spread share four traits: you can play instantly with no login, the interaction itself feels good, every attempt earns a graded score rather than pass/fail, and a session is over in a few minutes so you come back tomorrow.
